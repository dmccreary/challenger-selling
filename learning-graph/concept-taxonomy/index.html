    Concept Taxonomy for Challenger Selling¶

    This document defines the categorical taxonomy for organizing the 401 concepts in the Challenger Selling learning graph.

    Taxonomy Categories¶

    CHAL - Challenger Sales Methodology¶

    TaxonomyID: CHAL Description: Core Challenger Sales methodology concepts including the three principles (teach, tailor, take control), sales profiles, insights, and stakeholder analysis.

    STORY - Storytelling Fundamentals¶

    TaxonomyID: STORY Description: Storytelling principles, narrative structure, psychology of memory and decision-making, and story components for sales.

    PSYCH - Psychology of Decision-Making¶

    TaxonomyID: PSYCH Description: Cognitive psychology concepts including memory types, neural coupling, decision fatigue, biases, and behavioral economics applied to sales.

    PERSONA - Buyer Personas and Segmentation¶

    TaxonomyID: PERSONA Description: Buyer persona definitions (CIO, CTO, CFO, CEO, etc.), industry verticals, and stakeholder analysis for tailoring sales approaches.

    OBJEC - Objection Handling¶

    TaxonomyID: OBJEC Description: Story-based objection handling strategies for price, timing, competitors, risk, and authority objections.

    PORTF - Story Portfolio Management¶

    TaxonomyID: PORTF Description: Managing story libraries, metadata, versioning, analytics, and organizing stories for different use cases.

    AIGEN - AI-Assisted Story Generation¶

    TaxonomyID: AIGEN Description: AI tools for story generation including LLMs, prompt engineering, human-AI collaboration, and ethical considerations.

    AGENT - Interactive Sales Agents¶

    TaxonomyID: AGENT Description: Conversational AI, chatbots, dialogue systems, intent recognition, and sales simulation tools.

    GRAPH - Graph Database Fundamentals¶

    TaxonomyID: GRAPH Description: Graph database concepts including data models, nodes/edges, property graphs, LPG graphs, query languages, and use cases.

    GSALE - Graph Database Sales Stories¶

    TaxonomyID: GSALE Description: Specific sales stories and case studies for graph databases, including comparisons with relational databases and implementation stories.

    AIFRAME - AI Framework Fundamentals¶

    TaxonomyID: AIFRAME Description: Machine learning and deep learning frameworks (TensorFlow, PyTorch, etc.), neural networks, MLOps, and AI implementation.

    AISALE - AI Framework Sales Stories¶

    TaxonomyID: AISALE Description: Sales stories and case studies for AI frameworks, including adoption stories, ROI, and success cases.

    ITEXT - Intelligent Textbook Tools¶

    TaxonomyID: ITEXT Description: Educational technology, adaptive learning, learning analytics, xAPI, LRS, MicroSims, and intelligent textbook generation.

    ITSALE - Intelligent Textbook Sales Stories¶

    TaxonomyID: ITSALE Description: Sales stories for intelligent textbook tools, including educational transformation, student engagement, and learning outcome stories.

    METRIC - Story Effectiveness Measurement¶

    TaxonomyID: METRIC Description: Measuring story impact, engagement metrics, retention, conversion, A/B testing, and sales cycle analysis.

    DELIV - Story Delivery and Integration¶

    TaxonomyID: DELIV Description: Multi-channel story delivery, CRM integration, sales enablement platforms, and automation tools.

    COACH - Story-Based Coaching¶

    TaxonomyID: COACH Description: Sales team training, workshops, peer sharing, best practices, feedback loops, and performance reviews.

    LIBRY - Story Libraries and Customization¶

    TaxonomyID: LIBRY Description: Organizing story libraries by industry, persona, objection, stage, and product; dynamic adaptation and context-aware storytelling.

    TRUST - Story Trust Building¶

    TaxonomyID: TRUST Description: Building credibility, authority, vulnerability, authenticity, and ethical storytelling practices.

    ETHIC - Story Ethics and Compliance¶

    TaxonomyID: ETHIC Description: Truth in storytelling, exaggeration avoidance, customer privacy, transparency, and legal compliance.

    CREATE - Story Creation Process¶

    TaxonomyID: CREATE Description: End-to-end story creation workflow from ideation through deployment, maintenance, and retirement.

    TEMPL - Story Templates and Frameworks¶

    TaxonomyID: TEMPL Description: Modular story components, building blocks, patterns, frameworks, scripting, storyboarding, and visualization.

    DASHB - Story Analytics Dashboard¶

    TaxonomyID: DASHB Description: Performance tracking, ROI calculation, benchmarking, competitive analysis, and innovation measurement.

    INNOV - Story Innovation and Strategy¶

    TaxonomyID: INNOV Description: Story differentiation, brand alignment, corporate guidelines, governance, and strategic planning.

    CRISIS - Story Crisis Management¶

    TaxonomyID: CRISIS Description: Crisis communication, reputation management, damage control, recovery, and learning from failure.

    IMPRO - Story Continuous Improvement¶

    TaxonomyID: IMPRO Description: Innovation labs, experimentation, A/B testing, user research, customer interviews, and trend analysis.

    STRAT - Story Strategic Planning¶

    TaxonomyID: STRAT Description: Roadmap development, resource allocation, budget planning, team structure, skill development, and knowledge management.

    SUCCE - Story Success Metrics¶

    TaxonomyID: SUCCE Description: KPIs, OKRs, balanced scorecards, dashboards, reporting, executive summaries, and business reviews.

    ALIGN - Story Strategic Alignment¶

    TaxonomyID: ALIGN Description: Business value, ROI models, cost-benefit analysis, investment prioritization, portfolio management, and resource optimization.

    ARCH - Story Integration Architecture¶

    TaxonomyID: ARCH Description: Technology stack, data architecture, security, privacy, compliance, scalability, performance, reliability, and availability.

    DISAS - Story Disaster Recovery¶

    TaxonomyID: DISAS Description: Business continuity, incident management, problem management, change management, release management, and configuration management.

    DEVOP - Story DevOps and Testing¶

    TaxonomyID: DEVOP Description: Asset management, version control, CI/CD, testing strategy, quality assurance, user acceptance testing, and accessibility testing.

    AIADV - Advanced AI for Stories¶

    TaxonomyID: AIADV Description: Natural language processing, computer vision, speech recognition, translation, sentiment analysis, emotion detection, and pattern recognition.

    IMM - Immersive Story Experiences¶

    TaxonomyID: IMM Description: Virtual reality, augmented reality, mixed reality, immersive experiences, gamification, and serious games.

    CAP - Capstone Project¶

    TaxonomyID: CAP Description: The culminating project that integrates all course concepts into a comprehensive story-based sales strategy.
