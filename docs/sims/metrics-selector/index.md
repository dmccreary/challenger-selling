## About This MicroSim

"Are our stories working?" is really four different questions. Are customers paying attention in the meeting (**engagement**)? Do they remember the insight weeks later (**retention**)? Are we winning more deals (**conversion**)? Are deals moving faster (**pipeline velocity**)?

This MicroSim gives you four business objectives and asks you to pick the metric family that answers each one. Feedback explains what each metric measures and why it fits. The summary reminds you that leading indicators and business outcomes belong together on the same dashboard.

**Learning objective (Apply, *select*):** Select appropriate story impact metrics (Engagement, Retention, Conversion, Pipeline Velocity) for different business objectives.

## How to Use

1. Read the business objective.
2. Choose one of the four **Metric** buttons.
3. Select **Submit** for feedback. You can resubmit; only the first try counts.
4. Select **Next Objective**. After all four, the summary lists each objective and its metric.

## Lesson Plan

### Audience
Sales professionals, account executives, sales engineers and business development managers.

### Duration
8 minutes

### Prerequisites
- Story Impact Metrics section of Chapter 9

### Activities

1. **Matching** (4 min): Complete all four objectives and record your first-try score.
2. **Classification** (2 min): Sort the four metrics into leading indicators and business outcomes.
3. **Transfer** (2 min): Name one specific measurement your team could collect next week for each metric family.

### Common Misconceptions
- All metrics are equally important.
- One metric tells the whole story.
- Metrics are only for reporting.

### Assessment
- Correct metric for all four objectives.
- Learner can explain the difference between conversion and pipeline velocity.
