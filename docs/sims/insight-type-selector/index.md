## About This MicroSim

Insights land differently depending on the customer's mindset. A confident market leader needs a **Warmer** insight showing that competitors are quietly moving ahead. An overwhelmed buyer needs a **Rational Drowning** insight that cuts the decision down to the few factors that matter. An ambitious customer responds to a **Rock Star** insight that shows the path to top-tier performance.

In this MicroSim you read three customer scenarios, choose the insight type for each, and briefly explain why. After each submission you see an example of the right insight type written for that customer, alongside your explanation, so you can compare your reasoning with a worked example.

**Learning objective (Apply, *select*):** Select the appropriate insight type (Warmer, Rational Drowning, Rock Star) for different customer situations and explain why that type is most effective.

## How to Use

1. Read the customer scenario.
2. Choose an **Insight Type** and write a brief explanation.
3. Select **Submit** to see feedback and an example insight. You can resubmit; only your first try counts.
4. Select **Next Scenario**. After all three, the summary shows when to use each type.

## Lesson Plan

### Audience
Sales professionals, account executives, sales engineers and business development managers.

### Duration
10 minutes

### Prerequisites
- Insight Types section of Chapter 1

### Activities

1. **Selection** (5 min): Complete all three scenarios with explanations.
2. **Rewrite** (3 min): Rewrite the Scenario 1 example as a Rock Star insight. Why does it work less well for a complacent leader?
3. **Transfer** (2 min): Which insight type fits your most important prospect right now?

### Common Misconceptions
- All insight types work equally well in all situations.
- Warmer insights are always the best.
- Rock Star insights are arrogant.

### Assessment
- Correct insight type for all three scenarios.
- Explanations name the customer mindset that drives the choice.
