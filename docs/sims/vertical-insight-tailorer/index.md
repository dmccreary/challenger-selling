## About This MicroSim

A Challenger insight is only as strong as its relevance. The fact that legacy data systems eat 40% of an IT budget is true in every industry, but it *means* something different in each one. In a hospital it means less investment in clinical tools and fragmented patient data. In a bank it means aging platforms under regulatory scrutiny. At a software company it means engineers maintaining instead of shipping.

In this MicroSim you tailor that single base insight for three verticals. For each one you choose a **framing** and a **messaging focus**. After you submit you see your choice rewritten as an actual insight, next to the strongest adaptation. Weak framings stay in plain view, so you can see why "patient outcomes" falls flat with a bank.

**Learning objective (Apply, *adapt*):** Adapt a Challenger insight for different industry verticals by selecting the appropriate framing and messaging focus.

## How to Use

1. Read the base insight at the top.
2. For the current vertical, choose a **Framing** and a **Messaging Focus**.
3. Select **Submit** to see how your choice reads as an insight and how it compares to the strongest adaptation. You can resubmit with different choices.
4. Select **Next Vertical** to continue. The summary shows all three adapted insights side by side.

## Lesson Plan

### Audience
Sales professionals, account executives, sales engineers and business development managers.

### Duration
10-15 minutes

### Prerequisites
- Industry Vertical Segmentation sections of Chapter 3

### Activities

1. **Tailoring** (5 min): Complete all three verticals and record your first-try score.
2. **Comparison** (4 min): In the summary, which words changed between the three adapted insights, and which stayed the same?
3. **Transfer** (5 min): Write a fourth adaptation of the base insight for the Manufacturing vertical (hint: downtime and throughput).

### Common Misconceptions
- The same insight works across all industries.
- Industry differences are superficial.
- Vertical expertise isn't necessary.

### Assessment
- Correct framing and focus for all three verticals.
- A learner-written Manufacturing adaptation that uses operational-efficiency language and a concrete metric.
