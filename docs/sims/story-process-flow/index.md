## About This MicroSim

A story moves through eight stages, from ideation to retirement. **Gates** between stages decide whether it is ready to move on. Skipping stages is how untested or outdated stories end up in front of customers.

In this MicroSim you click the eight shuffled stages into order. Misplaced stages are marked, with a hint about the first one. Once the sequence is correct, three gate questions appear: what must exist before a story moves from ideation to drafting, from refinement to testing, and from testing to approval. Answering them draws the full process flow, including the loop back to refinement when a test fails.

**Learning objective (Understand, *explain*):** Explain the story creation process by identifying the correct sequence of stages and the gate criteria between stages.

## How to Use

1. Click the stages in **Available stages** in the order a story moves through them.
2. Click a placed stage to send it back. Select **Clear** to start over.
3. Select **Check Sequence**. Fix any stage marked &#10007; and check again.
4. Answer the three gate questions, then select **Check Gates &amp; Show Flow** to see the diagram.

## Lesson Plan

### Audience
Sales professionals, account executives, sales engineers and business development managers.

### Duration
10 minutes

### Prerequisites
- Story Creation Process section of Chapter 12

### Activities

1. **Sequencing** (4 min): Put the eight stages in order. How many tries did it take?
2. **Gates** (3 min): Answer the gate questions and study the flow diagram.
3. **Discussion** (3 min): Which stage does your team skip most often, and what problems does that cause?

### Common Misconceptions
- Stages can be skipped.
- Testing is optional.
- Deployment is the end.

### Assessment
- Correct sequence of all eight stages.
- Correct criteria for all three gates.
