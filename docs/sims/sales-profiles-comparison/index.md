## About This MicroSim

CEB research on more than 6,000 B2B sales reps found five sales profiles: Relationship Builder, Hard Worker, Lone Wolf, Reactive Problem Solver, and Challenger. Reps were spread fairly evenly across the profiles. The striking difference appeared among star performers.

In this MicroSim you click each profile card to reveal its characteristics, strength, and weakness. Once all five are open, a chart shows each profile's share of star performers in complex sales: Challengers 54%, Relationship Builders just 4%. The figures come from CEB research published in "The End of Solution Sales," *Harvard Business Review* (July–August 2012). A three-question quiz in random order then checks the key comparisons, with one attempt per question.

**Learning objective (Understand, *compare*):** Compare the five sales profiles (Relationship Builder, Hard Worker, Lone Wolf, Reactive Problem Solver, Challenger) and identify which profile correlates with high performance in complex sales.

## How to Use

1. Click each of the five profile cards to reveal it.
2. Study the performance chart that appears.
3. Answer the three quiz questions. Each allows one attempt.
4. Review your score, then select **Retry Quiz** for a new random order.

## Lesson Plan

### Audience
Sales professionals, account executives, sales engineers and business development managers.

### Duration
8 minutes

### Prerequisites
- Sales Profile definitions in Chapter 1

### Activities

1. **Exploration** (3 min): Reveal all five profiles and study the chart.
2. **Quiz** (2 min): Answer the three questions; aim for 3 of 3 on the first attempt.
3. **Reflection** (3 min): Which profile best describes how you sell today? Which Challenger behavior would you develop first?

### Common Misconceptions
- Relationship Builders are the highest performers.
- Hard Workers achieve the best results through effort alone.
- All profiles can achieve the same results.

### Assessment
- 3 of 3 quiz questions correct on the first attempt.
- Learner can explain why Relationship Builders underperform in complex sales.
