## About This MicroSim

A **metrics cascade** links a business objective to the storytelling objectives that drive it, and each storytelling objective to the KPIs that track it. Without the cascade, teams measure whatever is easy (page views, story counts) instead of what matters.

In this MicroSim the business objective is to increase Q4 revenue by 20%. You choose two or three storytelling objectives (choosing all four is flagged as a loss of focus), then one or two KPIs for each. Some KPI options belong to a different objective; others are vanity metrics. Building the cascade draws a three-tier diagram with each link marked aligned or misaligned.

**Learning objective (Apply, *design*):** Design a metrics cascade by linking business objectives to storytelling objectives to specific KPIs.

## How to Use

1. Read the business objective.
2. Step 1: check two or three storytelling objectives.
3. Step 2: for each objective, check one or two KPIs.
4. Select **Build Cascade** to see feedback and the diagram. Swap flagged KPIs and rebuild.

## Lesson Plan

### Audience
Sales professionals, account executives, sales engineers and business development managers.

### Duration
10 minutes

### Prerequisites
- Story Success Metrics and Story KPIs sections of Chapter 14

### Activities

1. **Design** (5 min): Build an aligned cascade with three objectives.
2. **Critique** (2 min): Which KPI options are vanity metrics, and why?
3. **Transfer** (3 min): Draw a cascade for your team's current revenue goal.

### Common Misconceptions
- Any metric is a KPI.
- Metrics don't need to align.
- More metrics is better.

### Assessment
- Two or three objectives, each with only aligned KPIs.
- Learner can explain the difference between an activity metric and an outcome KPI.
