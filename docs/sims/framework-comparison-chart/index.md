## About This MicroSim

When a prospect's data team names its machine learning framework, it tells you a lot about where they are in their AI journey. **Scikit-learn** signals traditional ML and quick prototyping. **PyTorch** signals research and experimentation. **TensorFlow** signals production systems at scale.

This MicroSim keeps a three-row comparison chart (strengths, best use, skill level) on screen while you answer three scenario questions. Each answer is tied back to a row of the chart, and the summary reinforces the rule that no framework is best for everything.

**Learning objective (Understand, *compare*):** Compare AI/ML frameworks (TensorFlow, PyTorch, Scikit-learn) based on their characteristics and use cases.

## How to Use

1. Review the comparison chart at the top.
2. Read the question and choose a framework button.
3. Select **Submit** for feedback. You can change your answer and resubmit; only the first try counts.
4. Select **Next Question**. After all three, the summary lists the selection rule for each scenario.

## Lesson Plan

### Audience
Sales professionals, account executives, sales engineers and business development managers.

### Duration
8 minutes

### Prerequisites
- Machine Learning Frameworks section of Chapter 7

### Activities

1. **Review** (2 min): Read the chart and predict which row each kind of team (startup, research lab, enterprise) will lean on.
2. **Questions** (3 min): Answer all three questions and record your first-try score.
3. **Transfer** (3 min): A prospect says "we just moved our models from PyTorch to TensorFlow Serving." What does that tell you about their stage, and which insight would you lead with?

### Common Misconceptions
- All frameworks are the same.
- One framework is best for everything.
- Frameworks are only for data scientists.

### Assessment
- All three framework questions correct.
- Learner can name one strength of each framework.
