## About This MicroSim

A story can be true, well written, and still describe the wrong brand. This MicroSim gives you four brand attributes (innovation, reliability, customer focus, technical excellence) and a draft that celebrates being "conservative and risk-averse."

For each attribute you choose aligned, neutral, or misaligned and explain which words support your rating. The analysis puts your ratings and reasoning next to an expert review, suggests how to improve alignment on each attribute, and shows an on-brand rewrite that keeps the reliability message while adding innovation, customer success, and technical depth.

**Learning objective (Evaluate, *evaluate*):** Evaluate a story against brand attributes (innovation, reliability, customer focus, technical excellence) and identify alignment or misalignment.

## How to Use

1. Read the brand attributes and the story draft.
2. For each attribute, choose a rating and explain which words support it.
3. Select **Analyze Alignment** to compare your evaluation with the expert review.
4. Change any rating and resubmit to compare; your first-attempt score is kept.

## Lesson Plan

### Audience
Sales professionals, account executives, sales engineers and business development managers.

### Duration
12 minutes

### Prerequisites
- Story Brand Alignment and Brand Voice Consistency sections of Chapter 13

### Activities

1. **Evaluation** (6 min): Rate and explain all four attributes.
2. **Rewrite** (4 min): Write your own on-brand version before reading the expert rewrite.
3. **Discussion** (2 min): Why is "Customer Focus" neutral rather than aligned, even though customers are mentioned?

### Common Misconceptions
- Brand alignment is optional.
- Any story fits any brand.
- Alignment is about logos only.

### Assessment
- Ratings match the expert on all four attributes.
- Explanations cite specific words in the draft.
