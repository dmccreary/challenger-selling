## About This MicroSim

Stories only change behavior if reps can find them where they work, and if the team can see which stories win. That takes **integration**: connecting the systems that store stories, the system reps work in, and the system that measures outcomes.

In this MicroSim you pick three or four systems from a typical sales technology stack, then route three data flows: the story library, story usage data, and story performance. On submit, your architecture is drawn as a diagram. Systems you selected are blue, valid flows are green, and misrouted flows are red and dashed. Feedback explains each route, flags any missing core system, and points out the closed loop (Enablement &rarr; CRM &rarr; Analytics &rarr; Enablement) that makes integration ongoing work rather than a one-time project.

**Learning objective (Apply, *design*):** Design a story integration architecture by selecting which systems to integrate (CRM, LMS, CMS) and defining the data flow between them.

## How to Use

1. Step 1: check 3&ndash;4 systems to include in your architecture.
2. Step 2: choose a target system for each of the three data flows.
3. Select **Submit Architecture** to see the diagram and feedback.
4. Change any selections and submit again to compare designs.

## Lesson Plan

### Audience
Sales professionals, account executives, sales engineers and business development managers.

### Duration
10 minutes

### Prerequisites
- Story Integration, CRM Integration, and Sales Enablement Platforms sections of Chapter 10

### Activities

1. **Design** (4 min): Build a valid architecture and study the diagram.
2. **Experiment** (3 min): Add the LMS and route the story library there instead. What breaks?
3. **Transfer** (3 min): Map the real systems your company uses onto the three roles: library, workspace, and measurement.

### Common Misconceptions
- Integration is only about CRM.
- All integrations are the same.
- Integration is one-time.

### Assessment
- A valid architecture: CRM, Sales Enablement, and Analytics with all three flows routed correctly.
- Learner can explain why performance data must flow back to the enablement platform.
