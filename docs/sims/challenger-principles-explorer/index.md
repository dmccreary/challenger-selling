## About This MicroSim

The Challenger methodology rests on three principles. **Teach** reframes how the customer sees their business. **Tailor** adapts the insight to each stakeholder. **Take control** gives the decision structure and momentum.

In this MicroSim you click each principle card to reveal its description and a concrete example. Once all three are open, a diagram shows how they work together: teaching creates urgency, tailoring builds relevance, and taking control converts urgency into action. A three-question quiz in random order then asks which principle fits each situation. You get one attempt per question and can retry the quiz.

**Learning objective (Understand, *explain*):** Explain how the three Challenger principles (teach, tailor, take control) work together to drive customer action.

## How to Use

1. Click each of the three cards to reveal it.
2. Read the diagram of how the principles work together.
3. Answer the three quiz questions. Each allows one attempt.
4. Review your score, then select **Retry Quiz** for a new random order.

## Lesson Plan

### Audience
Sales professionals, account executives, sales engineers and business development managers.

### Duration
8 minutes

### Prerequisites
- Challenger Sales Methodology, Teaching Principle, Tailoring Principle, and Taking Control Principle sections of Chapter 1

### Activities

1. **Exploration** (3 min): Reveal all three principles and read the examples.
2. **Quiz** (2 min): Answer the three questions; aim for 3 of 3 on the first attempt.
3. **Discussion** (3 min): Describe a moment in a recent deal when taking control would have changed the outcome.

### Common Misconceptions
- The principles are sequential steps rather than integrated behaviors.
- Teaching means lecturing rather than sharing insights.
- Taking control means being pushy rather than providing structure.

### Assessment
- 3 of 3 quiz questions correct on the first attempt.
- Learner can explain why the principles are integrated behaviors rather than steps.
