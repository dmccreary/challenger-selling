## About This MicroSim

Storytelling budgets are limited, so every story initiative should support a business strategy. This MicroSim lists four strategies (market expansion, premium positioning, customer retention, operational efficiency) and four initiatives.

You map each initiative to the strategy it supports. The alignment matrix shows your mappings against the recommended ones and points out any strategy left without stories, because a strategy with no stories gets no help from the sales team.

**Learning objective (Apply, *align*):** Align storytelling objectives with business strategy by selecting appropriate storytelling initiatives for different strategic priorities.

## How to Use

1. Read the four business strategies.
2. Choose a strategy for each of the four initiatives.
3. Select **Show Alignment Matrix** to see feedback and the matrix.
4. Change any mapping and resubmit to compare.

## Lesson Plan

### Audience
Sales professionals, account executives, sales engineers and business development managers.

### Duration
8 minutes

### Prerequisites
- Story Strategic Alignment and Story Business Value sections of Chapter 14

### Activities

1. **Mapping** (4 min): Map all four initiatives and study the matrix.
2. **Gap analysis** (2 min): Map two initiatives to the same strategy. Which strategy is left uncovered?
3. **Transfer** (2 min): List your company's top strategy and one story initiative that would support it.

### Common Misconceptions
- Any story supports any strategy.
- Strategy doesn't affect storytelling.
- Alignment is one-time.

### Assessment
- All four initiatives mapped correctly.
- Learner can explain why alignment must be revisited as strategy changes.
