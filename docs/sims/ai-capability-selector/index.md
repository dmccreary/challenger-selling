## About This MicroSim

"AI" covers very different capabilities. **Speech recognition** turns audio into text. **NLP**, including natural language generation, understands and writes language. **Sentiment AI** classifies opinions as positive, negative, or neutral. **Emotion AI** reads emotional states from voice or facial cues.

This MicroSim gives you four storytelling use cases and asks you to choose the capability for each. Feedback explains what each capability does, and the summary shows how they often chain together in one workflow: transcribe, analyze, then draft.

**Learning objective (Apply, *select*):** Select appropriate AI capabilities (NLP, Speech Recognition, Sentiment AI, Emotion AI) for different storytelling use cases.

## How to Use

1. Read the use case.
2. Choose one of the four **AI Capability** buttons.
3. Select **Submit** for feedback. You can resubmit; only your first try counts.
4. Select **Next Use Case**. After all four, the summary lists each use case and its capability.

## Lesson Plan

### Audience
Sales professionals, account executives, sales engineers and business development managers.

### Duration
8 minutes

### Prerequisites
- Story Artificial Intelligence, Story Natural Language Processing, and Story Sentiment AI sections of Chapter 16

### Activities

1. **Matching** (4 min): Complete all four use cases and record your first-try score.
2. **Chaining** (2 min): Put the four capabilities in the order they would run when analyzing a recorded sales call.
3. **Ethics** (2 min): When should a customer be told that emotion AI is analyzing their voice?

### Common Misconceptions
- All AI is the same.
- AI replaces humans.
- Any AI works for any task.

### Assessment
- Correct capability for all four use cases.
- Learner can explain the difference between sentiment AI and emotion AI.
