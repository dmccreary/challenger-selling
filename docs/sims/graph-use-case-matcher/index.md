## About This MicroSim

Graph databases earn their keep on problems where the answer lives in the **connections**, not in any single record. This MicroSim gives you four realistic business scenarios: a bank chasing fraud rings, a social platform looking for influencers, an e-commerce site recommending products, and a pharmaceutical company organizing research data. You pick the use case that fits each one.

Feedback explains which relationships carry the answer, and when you choose wrong it tells you what your choice *is* good for. Knowing these four patterns lets you recognize graph opportunities in a prospect's business and tell a story that matches.

**Learning objective (Apply, *identify*):** Identify which graph database use case (Social Network Analysis, Fraud Detection, Recommendation Engines, Knowledge Graphs) applies to different business scenarios.

## How to Use

1. Read the business scenario.
2. Choose one of the four **Graph Use Case** buttons.
3. Select **Submit** to see feedback. You can change your choice and resubmit; only your first try counts.
4. Select **Next Scenario**. After all four, a summary table shows each scenario and why its use case fits.

## Lesson Plan

### Audience
Sales professionals, account executives, sales engineers and business development managers.

### Duration
8 minutes

### Prerequisites
- Graph Use Cases section of Chapter 6

### Activities

1. **Matching** (4 min): Complete all four scenarios and record your first-try score.
2. **Reflection** (2 min): For the scenario you found hardest, name the relationship (edge) that carries the answer.
3. **Transfer** (2 min): Name one prospect in your territory that has one of these four problems.

### Common Misconceptions
- All use cases are the same.
- Any use case works for any problem.
- Use cases are technical only.

### Assessment
- Correct use case for all four scenarios.
- Learner can explain why fraud detection needs a graph even when every individual account looks normal.
