## About This MicroSim

Every stakeholder in a complex sale filters your message through their own priorities. A CIO hears "strategic positioning," a CFO hears "payback period," and a VP of Sales hears "win rates." Sending the same message to all three wastes the meeting.

In this MicroSim you read three short stakeholder descriptions. For each one you pick the **persona** from the seven defined in the chapter (CIO, CTO, CFO, CEO, VP of Sales, IT Director, Procurement Manager) and the **messaging focus** that fits that persona. Feedback explains the match, and when you choose wrong it tells you which persona your choice *would* suit. The descriptions deliberately avoid job titles, because priorities, not titles, define a persona.

**Learning objective (Apply, *identify*):** Identify the correct buyer persona from a description and select the appropriate messaging strategy for that persona.

## How to Use

1. Read the stakeholder description.
2. Choose a **Persona** and a **Messaging Focus** from the two dropdowns.
3. Select **Submit** to see feedback. You can change your selections and submit again to compare explanations; only your first try counts toward the score.
4. Select **Next Stakeholder** to continue. After all three, a summary table shows each persona-to-message mapping.

## Lesson Plan

### Audience
Sales professionals, account executives, sales engineers and business development managers.

### Duration
10 minutes

### Prerequisites
- Executive Personas and Functional Personas sections of Chapter 3

### Activities

1. **Matching** (4 min): Complete all three descriptions. Record your first-try score.
2. **Reflection** (3 min): Description 1 mentions ROI, yet it is a CIO and not a CFO. Which phrase gives it away?
3. **Transfer** (3 min): Write a one-sentence description of a real stakeholder from one of your accounts, then name the persona and messaging focus.

### Common Misconceptions
- All executives care about the same things.
- Job titles alone define personas.
- One message works for all personas.

### Assessment
- Correct persona and messaging focus for all three descriptions.
- Learner can explain why a CIO wants strategic positioning rather than technical architecture.
