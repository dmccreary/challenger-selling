## About This MicroSim

Customization is a trade-off. Deep personalization makes a story land, but it takes research time that is only worth spending on the right relationships. Light customization is fast but often feels generic.

In this MicroSim you customize one base story for three buyers. For each one you choose which elements to adapt (industry reference, persona language, specific examples) and the personalization level. The story is rewritten from your choices so you can compare light, medium, and deep versions side by side. The goal is to match depth to the buyer, not to always go deep.

**Learning objective (Apply, *adapt*):** Adapt a base story for different customers by selecting customization elements (industry reference, persona language, specific examples) and adjusting the level of personalization.

## How to Use

1. Read the base story and the buyer description.
2. Choose what to **Customize** and a **Personalization Level**.
3. Select **Submit** to see the customized story and feedback. Try other levels and resubmit to compare; only your first try counts.
4. Select **Next Buyer**. After all three, the summary shows the best choices for each buyer.

## Lesson Plan

### Audience
Sales professionals, account executives, sales engineers and business development managers.

### Duration
10 minutes

### Prerequisites
- Story Customization and Dynamic Story Adaptation sections of Chapter 11

### Activities

1. **Customizing** (5 min): Complete all three buyers and record your first-try score.
2. **Comparison** (2 min): Submit the CFO story at Light, Medium, and Deep. Which would you send to a busy CFO, and why?
3. **Transfer** (3 min): Choose a customization level for each of your top three open opportunities and justify it.

### Common Misconceptions
- The same story works for everyone.
- Deep personalization is always better.
- Customization is only about names.

### Assessment
- Correct elements and level for all three buyers.
- Learner can explain why deep personalization is not always the best choice.
