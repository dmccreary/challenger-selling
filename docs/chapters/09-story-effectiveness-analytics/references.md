# References: Story Effectiveness & Analytics

1. [A/B Testing](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/A/B_testing) - Wikipedia - Statistical foundations, design considerations, and pitfalls for comparing story versions in sales experiments.

2. [Conversion Rate Optimization](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Conversion_rate_optimization) - Wikipedia - Methodologies for measuring and improving the rate at which prospects take desired actions after a story.

3. [Sales Process](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sales_process) - Wikipedia - Pipeline stages and metrics used to attribute story impact to cycle length, deal size, and close rate.

4. *Lean Analytics: Use Data to Build a Better Startup Faster* - Alistair Croll and Benjamin Yoskovitz - O'Reilly Media - Croll and Yoskovitz's lean measurement framework helps teams choose the right metrics, run experiments, and iterate on messaging.

5. *Data-Driven Marketing: The 15 Metrics Everyone in Marketing Should Know* - Mark Jeffery - Wiley - Jeffery's practical guide to attribution, ROI, and testing provides a sales-relevant foundation for measuring story effectiveness.

6. [Gong: Conversation Intelligence Research](https://www.gong.io/research/) - Gong.io - Data-driven studies of sales conversations, including talk patterns, question ratios, and story impact on win rates.

7. [HubSpot: Sales Metrics That Matter](https://blog.hubspot.com/sales/sales-metrics) - HubSpot - Guide to pipeline velocity, conversion rates, and other KPIs for evaluating sales content.

8. [Salesforce: Sales Analytics](https://www.salesforce.com/products/einstein/sales-analytics/) - Salesforce - Platform for building dashboards that track pipeline, rep activity, and content-influenced revenue.

9. [Harvard Business Review: Making Sales Metrics Matter](https://hbr.org/2015/06/making-sales-metrics-matter) - Harvard Business Review - Advice on selecting actionable metrics and avoiding metric overload in sales organizations.

10. [McKinsey: The Value of Data and Analytics](https://www.mckinsey.com/capabilities/quantumblack/our-insights/the-state-of-ai) - McKinsey - Research on data-driven decision making and AI-enabled analytics that supports predictive story analytics.
