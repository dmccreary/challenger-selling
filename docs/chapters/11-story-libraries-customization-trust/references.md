# References: Story Libraries, Customization & Trust

1. [Content Management System](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Content_management_system) - Wikipedia - Describes repositories, metadata, versioning, and retrieval that form the basis of story libraries.

2. [Personalization](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Personalization) - Wikipedia - Explains how adaptive systems tailor content to user context, preferences, and behavior.

3. [Trust (Social)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Trust_(social)) - Wikipedia - Social and psychological foundations of trust-building through credibility, reliability, and authenticity.

4. *The Trusted Advisor* - David H. Maister, Charles H. Green, and Robert M. Galford - Free Press - Maister, Green, and Galford's trust equation (credibility, reliability, intimacy, self-orientation) is a foundational model for consultative selling relationships.

5. *They Ask, You Answer: A Revolutionary Approach to Inbound Sales, Content, and Today's Digital Consumer* - Marcus Sheridan - Wiley - Sheridan pioneered using transparent, educational content to build trust and preempt objections in complex sales.

6. [Highspot: Sales Content Management](https://www.highspot.com/sales-content-management/) - Highspot - Best practices for organizing, tagging, and surfacing sales content at the right moment.

7. [Seismic: Content Personalization](https://seismic.com/platform/content-personalization/) - Seismic - Guidance on dynamically tailoring sales content for personas, industries, and deal stages.

8. [Salesforce: Content Management in Sales Cloud](https://www.salesforce.com/products/sales-cloud/features/crm-content/) - Salesforce - How CRM-integrated libraries help reps find and customize the right story.

9. [HubSpot: Sales Content Strategy](https://blog.hubspot.com/marketing/sales-content) - HubSpot - Practical framework for building and maintaining a library of sales-ready stories and case studies.

10. [Edelman Trust Barometer](https://www.edelman.com/trust/2024/trust-barometer) - Edelman - Annual research on trust in institutions, business, and information sources that informs authentic storytelling strategy.
