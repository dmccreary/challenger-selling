# References: Story Architecture, DevOps & Testing

1. [DevOps](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/DevOps) - Wikipedia - Culture, practices, and toolsets for integrating development and operations to enable rapid, reliable delivery.

2. [Continuous Integration and Continuous Delivery](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/CI/CD) - Wikipedia - CI/CD pipelines that automate build, test, and deployment of storytelling systems and content.

3. [Software Testing](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Software_testing) - Wikipedia - Types of testing—functional, performance, security, accessibility—needed for reliable storytelling platforms.

4. *The Phoenix Project: A Novel About IT, DevOps, and Helping Your Business Win* - Gene Kim, Kevin Behr, and George Spafford - IT Revolution Press - Kim's narrative popularized DevOps principles of flow, feedback, and continual learning for technical teams.

5. *Continuous Delivery: Reliable Software Releases through Build, Test, and Deployment Automation* - Jez Humble and David Farley - Addison-Wesley - Humble and Farley's book established the canonical practices for automated pipelines and deployment automation.

6. [GitLab: What is DevOps?](https://about.gitlab.com/topics/devops/) - GitLab - Comprehensive guide to DevOps culture, CI/CD, security, and observability practices.

7. [Atlassian: CI/CD Pipelines](https://www.atlassian.com/continuous-delivery) - Atlassian - Tutorial on building continuous integration and delivery pipelines with practical examples.

8. [AWS: DevOps and CI/CD](https://aws.amazon.com/devops/) - AWS - Cloud-native DevOps services, patterns, and best practices for automation and release management.

9. [W3C: Web Accessibility Initiative](https://www.w3.org/WAI/) - W3C - Standards and testing guidance for making web-based storytelling content accessible to all learners.

10. [OWASP: Web Security Testing Guide](https://owasp.org/www-project-web-security-testing-guide/) - OWASP - Open methodology and checklist for testing the security of web applications that host sales stories and customer data.
