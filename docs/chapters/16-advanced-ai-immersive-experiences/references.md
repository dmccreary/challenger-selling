# References: Advanced AI & Immersive Experiences

1. [Natural Language Processing](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Natural_language_processing) - Wikipedia - Core NLP tasks—tokenization, parsing, sentiment, generation—that power advanced storytelling AI.

2. [Virtual Reality](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Virtual_reality) - Wikipedia - Hardware, software, and interaction principles for fully immersive storytelling environments.

3. [Augmented Reality](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Augmented_reality) - Wikipedia - Overlay of digital story elements onto the physical world and enterprise use cases for guided demos.

4. *Speech and Language Processing* - Dan Jurafsky and James H. Martin - Prentice Hall - Jurafsky and Martin's textbook is the standard reference for NLP fundamentals that underlie modern story generation and sentiment analysis.

5. *The Infinite Retina: Spatial Computing and the Future of Storytelling* - Robert Scoble and Shel Israel - Buzzstops - Scoble and Israel map the business and experiential potential of AR, VR, and mixed-reality storytelling.

6. [NVIDIA: AI for Speech and NLP](https://www.nvidia.com/en-us/ai-data-science/ai-for-speech/) - NVIDIA - Resources on speech recognition, NLP, and conversational AI pipelines for real-time story applications.

7. [Unity: Virtual Reality Development](https://unity.com/solutions/vr) - Unity - Tutorials and tools for building immersive VR/AR storytelling experiences.

8. [Google AR & VR](https://arvr.google.com/) - Google - Overview of augmented- and virtual-reality platforms, design guidelines, and developer tools.

9. [OpenAI: GPT Models and Capabilities](https://openai.com/research) - OpenAI - Research papers and documentation on large language models that generate, summarize, and personalize narrative content.

10. [Coursera: VR and AR Specialization](https://www.coursera.org/specializations/virtual-reality) - Coursera / University of London - Structured course on designing and developing virtual reality experiences for storytelling and training.
