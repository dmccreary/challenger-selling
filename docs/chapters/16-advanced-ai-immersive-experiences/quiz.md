# Quiz: Advanced AI & Immersive Experiences

Test your understanding of advanced AI capabilities, immersive technologies, and their applications in storytelling with these 10 review questions.

---

#### 1. Speech recognition technology converts:

<div class="upper-alpha" markdown>
1. Spoken language into text
2. Written text into spoken audio
3. Images into descriptive text
4. Text into translated text
</div>

??? question "Show Answer"
    The correct answer is **A**. Speech recognition converts spoken language into text, enabling transcription and voice-based interaction. Text-to-speech does the reverse, while computer vision and translation address other tasks.

    **Concept Tested:** Story Speech Recognition

#### 2. Which AI capability identifies specific emotional states such as joy, frustration, or concern in customer communications?

<div class="upper-alpha" markdown>
1. Sentiment AI
2. Natural Language Processing
3. Pattern Recognition
4. Emotion AI
</div>

??? question "Show Answer"
    The correct answer is **D**. Emotion AI goes beyond sentiment to detect specific emotions. Sentiment AI assesses positive/negative tone, NLP handles language understanding, and pattern recognition finds recurring structures.

    **Concept Tested:** Story Emotion AI


---

#### 3. What is the primary value of story simulation in sales training?

<div class="upper-alpha" markdown>
1. It eliminates the need for live customer meetings
2. It enables risk-free experimentation with different story approaches
3. It removes the need for human coaches
4. It prevents all future objections
</div>

??? question "Show Answer"
    The correct answer is **C**. Simulation lets salespeople test approaches and see simulated customer responses without risking real deals. It complements rather than replaces live meetings and coaching.

    **Concept Tested:** Story Simulation


---

#### 4. A company wants to transcribe recorded sales calls into text for later story analysis. Which AI capability should it use?

<div class="upper-alpha" markdown>
1. Natural Language Processing
2. Speech Recognition
3. Sentiment AI
4. Emotion AI
</div>

??? question "Show Answer"
    The correct answer is **B**. Speech recognition transcribes audio into text. NLP could then analyze the text, but transcription is the first step that speech recognition provides.

    **Concept Tested:** Story Speech Recognition


---

#### 5. Analyzing customer email responses to determine whether the overall tone is positive, negative, or neutral is an application of:

<div class="upper-alpha" markdown>
1. Speech Recognition
2. Emotion AI
3. Sentiment AI
4. Anomaly Detection
</div>

??? question "Show Answer"
    The correct answer is **C**. Sentiment AI classifies the emotional tone of text as positive, negative, or neutral. Emotion AI identifies specific emotions, speech recognition handles audio, and anomaly detection finds unusual patterns.

    **Concept Tested:** Story Sentiment AI


---

#### 6. A manufacturing company wants prospective customers to take a virtual walkthrough of a new production line. Which immersive technology is most appropriate?

<div class="upper-alpha" markdown>
1. Augmented Reality that overlays data on a physical product
2. Mixed Reality that manipulates virtual objects in a real room
3. Text-to-Speech narration only
4. Virtual Reality that creates a fully immersive 3D facility tour
</div>

??? question "Show Answer"
    The correct answer is **D**. Virtual Reality creates a fully immersive three-dimensional environment, making it ideal for facility tours. AR and MR are better when digital elements must interact with the physical world, and TTS is audio-only.

    **Concept Tested:** Story Virtual Reality


---

#### 7. A story's conversion rate suddenly drops far below its historical baseline. Which AI capability would most likely flag this issue?

<div class="upper-alpha" markdown>
1. Forecasting
2. Anomaly Detection
3. Classification
4. Clustering
</div>

??? question "Show Answer"
    The correct answer is **B**. Anomaly Detection identifies unusual patterns that deviate from expected behavior, such as a sudden performance drop. Forecasting predicts future values, classification categorizes content, and clustering groups similar items.

    **Concept Tested:** Story Anomaly Detection

#### 8. Augmented Reality is most appropriate when the storytelling goal is to:

<div class="upper-alpha" markdown>
1. Overlay digital story elements onto the customer's physical environment
2. Immerse the customer in a completely separate virtual world
3. Replace all physical objects with digital equivalents
4. Deliver audio-only narratives
</div>

??? question "Show Answer"
    The correct answer is **A**. AR overlays digital information onto the real world, bridging physical and digital storytelling. VR creates separate immersive worlds, and audio-only delivery is not AR.

    **Concept Tested:** Story Augmented Reality

#### 9. A story digital twin of a customer's decision environment is primarily used to:

<div class="upper-alpha" markdown>
1. Conduct what-if analysis and optimize stories for that specific account
2. Replace the customer relationship management system
3. Eliminate the need for sales conversations
4. Store all customer data without analysis
</div>

??? question "Show Answer"
    The correct answer is **A**. A digital twin models the customer's context and decision process, enabling scenario testing and account-specific optimization. It complements rather than replaces CRM and live conversations.

    **Concept Tested:** Story Digital Twin

#### 10. A sales enablement team wants to teach objection handling through interactive challenges and achievement tracking. Which approach should they design?

<div class="upper-alpha" markdown>
1. A 360-degree virtual facility tour
2. A serious game or gamified simulation
3. Automated text-to-speech story narration
4. Real-time language translation
</div>

??? question "Show Answer"
    The correct answer is **B**. Serious games and gamified simulations use challenges, feedback, and rewards to teach skills interactively. VR tours, narration, and translation do not provide the interactive skill-practice environment described.

    **Concept Tested:** Story Serious Games
