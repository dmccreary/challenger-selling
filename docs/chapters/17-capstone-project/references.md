# References: Capstone Project

1. [Project-Based Learning](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Project-based_learning) - Wikipedia - Pedagogical approach where learners synthesize knowledge through extended, real-world projects like the capstone sales strategy.

2. [Intelligent Tutoring System](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Intelligent_tutoring_system) - Wikipedia - Adaptive, AI-powered educational systems similar to the intelligent textbook platform sold in the capstone scenario.

3. [Sales Process](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sales_process) - Wikipedia - End-to-end sales stages that the capstone project maps to stories, metrics, and implementation roadmaps.

4. *The Challenger Customer: Selling to the Hidden Influencer Who Can Multiply Your Results* - Brent Adamson, Matthew Dixon, Nick Toman, and Rick Delisi - Portfolio/Penguin - The authors extended Challenger research to focus on mobilizers and consensus selling, directly relevant to the capstone stakeholder strategy.

5. *The Art of Possibility: Transforming Professional and Personal Life* - Rosamund Stone Zander and Benjamin Zander - Penguin Books - The Zanders' framework for reframing problems and creating expansive narratives supports the capstone's insight-development and coaching design.

6. [Educause: Horizon Report](https://www.educause.edu/research-and-publications/research/educause-horizon-report) - Educause - Annual analysis of higher-ed technology trends that informs the university-customer context in the capstone.

7. [Training Industry: Learning Technologies](https://trainingindustry.com/magazine/nov-dec-2023/) - Training Industry - Research and case studies on corporate learning technology adoption and vendor selection.

8. [Gartner: EdTech Market Guide](https://www.gartner.com/en/newsroom/press-releases/2023-08-22-gartner-says-global-education-sector-technology-spend-to-grow-15-percent-in-2024) - Gartner - Market data and forecasts for education-technology spending that support the capstone business case.

9. [LinkedIn Learning: Sales Strategy](https://www.linkedin.com/learning/sales-strategy) - LinkedIn Learning - Video courses on building strategic account plans, value propositions, and sales presentations.

10. [Harvard Business Review: The Future of Sales](https://hbr.org/2021/02/the-future-of-sales-is-hybrid) - Harvard Business Review - Research on hybrid, digital-first selling that shapes the capstone delivery and AI-augmentation strategy.
