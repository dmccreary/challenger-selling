## Project Overview

Your capstone project is to design a complete sales strategy for selling an intelligent textbook generation platform to a specific customer. You will apply all the concepts you've learned: Challenger methodology, storytelling fundamentals, AI tools, analytics, delivery strategies, and creation processes.

The intelligent textbook generation platform enables organizations to create interactive, AI-powered educational content with MicroSims, learning graphs, and adaptive learning features. Your customer is either a university looking to modernize their course materials or a corporate training department seeking to improve employee training effectiveness.

## Project Requirements

### Part 1: Customer Analysis

Select a specific customer type (university or corporate training department) and research their context. Create a comprehensive customer analysis that includes:

**Industry and Context:**
- Industry trends and challenges
- Competitive landscape
- Regulatory or compliance requirements
- Recent initiatives or strategic priorities

**Stakeholder Mapping:**
- Identify all key stakeholders (CFO, CTO, academic leaders, training directors, instructors, etc.)
- For each stakeholder, document their priorities, metrics, and concerns
- Create a Customer Priority Map showing how different stakeholders prioritize competing concerns

**Persona Profiles:**
- Develop detailed profiles for 3-5 key personas you'll need to engage
- For each persona, include: role, objectives, challenges, decision criteria, and communication preferences

### Part 2: Challenger Insight Development

Develop a compelling Challenger insight that teaches your customer something new about their business. Your insight should:

- Reframe a problem the customer recognizes but hasn't solved
- Connect to a broader organizational outcome (financial, strategic, or operational)
- Be supported by data or research
- Be specific to your customer's industry and context

**Deliverable:**
- A clear statement of your Challenger insight
- The research or data that supports it
- Explanation of how it reframes the customer's thinking

### Part 3: Story Portfolio Creation

Create a comprehensive story portfolio that supports your Challenger insight across the sales cycle. Your portfolio should include:

**Insight Stories:**
- 2-3 stories that deliver your Challenger insight to different stakeholders
- Each story should follow the components: hook, problem, agitation, solution, social proof, call to action

**Objection Stories:**
- Stories for 3-5 common objections (price, timing, competitors, risk, authority)
- Each objection story should show how another customer faced the same concern and overcame it

**Persona-Specific Stories:**
- Adapt your core insight story for 2-3 different personas
- Show how you emphasize different aspects of the insight for each stakeholder

**Stage-Specific Stories:**
- Stories for different stages: awareness, consideration, evaluation, decision
- Ensure your story sequence builds logically across the sales cycle

### Part 4: Story Delivery Strategy

Design a comprehensive delivery strategy for your stories across channels and touchpoints:

**Multi-Channel Delivery:**
- Adapt your stories for email, presentations, video, and social media
- Document how each channel changes the story format and length

**Integration Plan:**
- Describe how you would integrate your story library into CRM and sales enablement platforms
- Outline the features that would support story selection, tracking, and analytics

**Coaching Plan:**
- Design a training program to teach salespeople to deliver your stories effectively
- Include workshop structure, practice activities, and feedback mechanisms

### Part 5: Analytics and Measurement

Design a measurement framework to track story effectiveness:

**Metrics Definition:**
- Define 5-7 key metrics that indicate story success
- Include both leading indicators (engagement) and lagging indicators (conversion, revenue)

**Dashboard Design:**
- Sketch a dashboard that would display these metrics
- Explain how the dashboard would guide storytelling decisions

**A/B Testing Plan:**
- Design an A/B test to compare two versions of your core story
- Define the hypothesis, test design, and success criteria

### Part 6: AI Augmentation

Describe how you would use AI to scale and enhance your storytelling:

**AI-Assisted Generation:**
- Explain how you would use AI to generate story variations
- Provide example prompts for AI story generation

**Interactive Agents:**
- Design a conversational agent for salespeople to practice objection handling
- Describe the agent's capabilities and how it would provide feedback

**Analytics AI:**
- Explain how AI could analyze story performance and suggest improvements
- Describe what patterns the AI might identify

### Part 7: Implementation Roadmap

Create a phased implementation plan for deploying your storytelling strategy:

**Phase 1: Foundation (Months 1-2)**
- Story library creation
- Integration with CRM
- Initial training

**Phase 2: Optimization (Months 3-4)**
- A/B testing and refinement
- Analytics implementation
- Advanced coaching

**Phase 3: Scale (Months 5-6)**
- AI augmentation
- Advanced personalization
- Continuous improvement

For each phase, include: objectives, activities, milestones, and success criteria.
