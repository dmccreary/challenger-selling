# References: Storytelling Fundamentals & Psychology

1. [Storytelling](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Storytelling) - Wikipedia - Comprehensive survey of narrative traditions, structure, and the psychological mechanisms that make stories memorable and persuasive.

2. [Hero's Journey](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hero%27s_journey) - Wikipedia - Detailed explanation of Joseph Campbell's monomyth stages and how the pattern maps to customer transformation stories.

3. [Narrative Transportation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Narrative_transportation) - Wikipedia - Covers how immersive stories change attitudes and intentions by reducing counter-arguing and increasing empathy.

4. *The Hero with a Thousand Faces* - Joseph Campbell - New World Library - Campbell originated the monomyth framework, which remains the standard lens for structuring customer-as-hero transformation narratives.

5. *Made to Stick: Why Some Ideas Survive and Others Die* - Chip Heath and Dan Heath - Random House - The Heath brothers' SUCCESs model (Simple, Unexpected, Concrete, Credible, Emotional, Stories) gives a practical, widely taught framework for making sales insights memorable.

6. [Pink: To Sell Is Human](https://www.penguinrandomhouse.com/books/309694/to-sell-is-human-by-daniel-h-pink/) - Daniel H. Pink - Pink's research-based argument that storytelling, clarity, and emotional resonance are central to modern persuasion and sales.

7. [TED: The Magical Science of Storytelling](https://www.ted.com/talks/david_jp_phillips_the_magical_science_of_storytelling) - TED - David JP Phillips explains how narrative structure and neurochemistry make stories more persuasive than facts alone.

8. [Why Your Brain Loves Good Storytelling](https://hbr.org/2014/10/why-your-brain-loves-good-storytelling) - Harvard Business Review - Brief research summary on neural coupling and oxytocin release during story listening.

9. [Psychology Today: The Power of Storytelling](https://www.psychologytoday.com/us/blog/brain-waves/201701/the-power-storytelling) - Psychology Today - Accessible overview of memory, empathy, and persuasion research tied to narrative.

10. [Annette Simmons: The Story Factor](https://www.annettesimmons.com/the-story-factor/) - Annette Simmons - Simmons is known for teaching business storytellers how to choose and deliver strategic stories that build trust and move audiences to action.
