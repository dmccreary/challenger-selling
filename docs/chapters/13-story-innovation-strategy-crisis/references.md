# References: Story Innovation, Strategy & Crisis

1. [Crisis Communication](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Crisis_communication) - Wikipedia - Frameworks for managing organizational reputation, messaging, and stakeholder trust during emergencies.

2. [Brand Management](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brand_management) - Wikipedia - Principles for maintaining brand voice, consistency, and alignment across all stories and channels.

3. [Innovation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Innovation) - Wikipedia - Theories of disruptive and incremental innovation that inform story differentiation and strategic narratives.

4. *The Innovator's Dilemma: When New Technologies Cause Great Firms to Fail* - Clayton M. Christensen - Harvard Business Review Press - Christensen's disruption framework provides a classic teaching insight for reframing competitive risk and technology adoption.

5. *Crisis Management: Planning for the Inevitable* - Steven Fink - iUniverse - Fink's lifecycle model (prodromal, acute, chronic, resolution) shaped modern crisis-communication planning and response storytelling.

6. [Harvard Business Review: How to Lead During a Crisis](https://hbr.org/topic/crisis-management) - Harvard Business Review - Curated articles on transparent communication, accountability, and recovery narratives.

7. [PRSA: Crisis Communication Resources](https://www.prsa.org/professional-development/sections/employee-communications/5-crisis-communication-tips) - PRSA - Guidance from the Public Relations Society of America on crisis messaging, spokesperson training, and stakeholder engagement.

8. [MIT Sloan: Leading Through Crisis](https://sloanreview.mit.edu/topic/crisis-management/) - MIT Sloan Management Review - Research-based articles on leadership communication and organizational resilience under pressure.

9. [Sprout Social: Crisis Communication Plan](https://sproutsocial.com/insights/crisis-communication-plan/) - Sprout Social - Practical templates for crisis response messages across social and digital channels.

10. [Gartner: Innovation Management](https://www.gartner.com/en/innovation-management) - Gartner - Research and frameworks for managing innovation pipelines, portfolios, and strategic differentiation narratives.
