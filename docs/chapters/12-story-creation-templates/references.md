# References: Story Creation & Templates

1. [Creative Writing](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Creative_writing) - Wikipedia - Craft elements—plot, character, conflict, and revision—that underpin effective story drafting.

2. [Screenwriting](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Screenwriting) - Wikipedia - Structural templates (three-act structure, beat sheets) and economy of scene that apply to short sales narratives.

3. [Template (File Format)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Template) - Wikipedia - Concept of reusable document structures and how templates standardize story creation workflows.

4. *Save the Cat! The Last Book on Screenwriting You'll Ever Need* - Blake Snyder - Michael Wiese Productions - Snyder's beat-sheet template became one of the most influential structures for crafting compact, emotionally satisfying stories.

5. *Story: Substance, Structure, Style, and the Principles of Screenwriting* - Robert McKee - Regan Books - McKee's seminar and book established a widely taught vocabulary for conflict, climax, and meaningful change in narrative.

6. [National Novel Writing Month: Plotting Resources](https://nanowrimo.org/) - NaNoWriMo - Community resources on brainstorming, drafting, and finishing creative projects under deadline.

7. [Writer's Digest: Story Structure](https://www.writersdigest.com/write-better-fiction/story-structure) - Writer's Digest - Articles on act structure, character arcs, and revision techniques applicable to sales stories.

8. [HubSpot: Storytelling Formula](https://blog.hubspot.com/marketing/storytelling-formula) - HubSpot - Copywriting frameworks and templates for concise business storytelling.

9. [Copyblogger: The Storytelling Process](https://copyblogger.com/storytelling/) - Copyblogger - Practical writing advice on hook, conflict, and resolution for marketing and sales narratives.

10. [Canva: Storyboard Templates](https://www.canva.com/templates/?search=storyboard) - Canva - Free visual storyboard templates that help teams plan and sequence sales narratives before writing.
