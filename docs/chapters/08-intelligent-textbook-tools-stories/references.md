# References: Intelligent Textbook Tools & Sales Stories

1. [Intelligent Tutoring System](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Intelligent_tutoring_system) - Wikipedia - Architecture, adaptivity, and pedagogical models behind AI-powered educational systems.

2. [Adaptive Learning](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Adaptive_learning) - Wikipedia - Methods for personalizing content paths, difficulty, and feedback based on learner performance.

3. [xAPI](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Experience_API) - Wikipedia - Overview of the Experience API standard, actor-verb-object statements, and Learning Record Stores.

4. *Learning Analytics: Theories, Processes, and Applications* - George Siemens - Wiley - Siemens, a founder of learning analytics, established the data-driven improvement framework that intelligent textbooks use to optimize content.

5. *The Cambridge Handbook of Multimedia Learning* - Richard E. Mayer (editor) - Cambridge University Press - Mayer's multimedia and cognitive-load principles provide the evidence base for designing effective MicroSims and interactive content.

6. [ADL xAPI Specification](https://xapi.com/specification/) - ADL Initiative - Official Experience API specification, documentation, and best practices for learning-record interoperability.

7. [Rustici Software: What is an LRS?](https://rusticisoftware.com/blog/what-is-a-learning-record-store/) - Rustici Software - Plain-language explanation of LRS architecture and how xAPI data feeds analytics.

8. [IMS Global: Learning Analytics](https://www.imsglobal.org/activity/learning-analytics) - 1EdTech - Standards and interoperability guidance for learning data, dashboards, and analytics in educational technology.

9. [Educause: Learning Analytics](https://www.educause.edu/research-and-publications/research/educause-horizon-report) - Educause - Horizon reports and research on adaptive learning, analytics, and the future of digital education.

10. [Harvard: Usable Learning](https://usablelearning.net/) - Justin Reich / Harvard Graduate School of Education - Research-based commentary on effective educational technology design, adaptive systems, and learning engineering.
