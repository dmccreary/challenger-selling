# References: Story Planning, Metrics & Alignment

1. [OKR](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/OKR) - Wikipedia - Goal-setting framework (Objectives and Key Results) used to align storytelling initiatives with business outcomes.

2. [Balanced Scorecard](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Balanced_scorecard) - Wikipedia - Strategic management framework that links financial, customer, process, and learning metrics.

3. [Return on Investment](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Return_on_investment) - Wikipedia - Formula and interpretation of ROI for storytelling programs, training, and technology investments.

4. *Measure What Matters: How Google, Bono, and the Gates Foundation Rock the World with OKRs* - John Doerr - Portfolio/Penguin - Doerr popularized OKRs as a method for aligning ambitious objectives with measurable key results across organizations.

5. *The Balanced Scorecard: Translating Strategy into Action* - Robert S. Kaplan and David P. Norton - Harvard Business Review Press - Kaplan and Norton created the balanced scorecard, the standard framework for measuring strategy across multiple perspectives.

6. [What Matters: OKR Resources](https://www.whatmatters.com/) - What Matters - Free OKR guides, examples, and templates for setting and tracking objectives and key results.

7. [Balanced Scorecard Institute](https://www.balancedscorecard.org/) - Balanced Scorecard Institute - Resources and training on building strategic scorecards and aligning metrics to strategy.

8. [HubSpot: Marketing ROI](https://www.hubspot.com/marketing-roi) - HubSpot - ROI calculator and methodology for measuring the return on marketing and sales content investments.

9. [Gartner: Strategic Planning](https://www.gartner.com/en/strategy/strategic-planning) - Gartner - Research on strategic alignment, portfolio prioritization, and investment decision making.

10. [APQC: Performance Measurement](https://www.apqc.org/resource-library/resource-listing/performance-measurement) - APQC - Benchmarks and best practices for designing metrics, dashboards, and performance-management systems.
