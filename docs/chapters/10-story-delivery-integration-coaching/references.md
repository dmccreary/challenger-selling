# References: Story Delivery, Integration & Coaching

1. [Sales Enablement](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sales_enablement) - Wikipedia - Defines the practices, content, and technologies that prepare sales teams to deliver insights and stories effectively.

2. [Customer Relationship Management](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Customer_relationship_management) - Wikipedia - Overview of CRM systems and how they store interactions, track content usage, and trigger story recommendations.

3. [Coaching](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Coaching) - Wikipedia - Principles of feedback, practice, and skill development that underpin story-based coaching programs.

4. *The Sales Acceleration Formula* - Mark Roberge - Wiley - Roberge's data-driven hiring, training, and coaching model at HubSpot became a widely adopted blueprint for scaling sales-team performance.

5. *Crucial Conversations: Tools for Talking When Stakes Are High* - Kerry Patterson, Joseph Grenny, Ron McMillan, and Al Switzler - McGraw-Hill - The authors' dialogue framework helps sellers deliver high-stakes Challenger insights with safety and persuasion.

6. [Salesforce: Sales Enablement Guide](https://www.salesforce.com/resources/articles/sales/sales-enablement/) - Salesforce - Best practices for equipping reps with content, training, and coaching to improve win rates.

7. [HubSpot: Sales Coaching](https://blog.hubspot.com/sales/sales-coaching) - HubSpot - Templates and methods for coaching reps on discovery, storytelling, and objection handling.

8. [Gartner: Sales Training Effectiveness](https://www.gartner.com/en/sales/research/sales-training) - Gartner - Research on what makes sales training and coaching programs stick and transfer to performance.

9. [Mindtickle: Sales Readiness](https://www.mindtickle.com/sales-readiness/) - Mindtickle - Platform guidance on onboarding, coaching, and reinforcement for sales messaging mastery.

10. [Showpad: Sales Content and Coaching](https://www.showpad.com/sales-enablement/) - Showpad - Resources on integrating content, training, and coaching into the sales workflow.
