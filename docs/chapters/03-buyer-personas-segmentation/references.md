# References: Buyer Personas & Industry Segmentation

1. [Buyer Persona](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Buyer_persona) - Wikipedia - Definition, research methods, and uses of persona profiles for tailoring messaging to decision-makers and influencers.

2. [Market Segmentation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Market_segmentation) - Wikipedia - Overview of demographic, firmographic, behavioral, and psychographic segmentation approaches used in B2B sales.

3. [Customer Segment](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Customer_segment) - Wikipedia - Describes how organizations group buyers by shared needs, priorities, and buying behaviors to focus sales and marketing efforts.

4. *Crossing the Chasm* - Geoffrey A. Moore - HarperBusiness - Moore's technology-adoption lifecycle and persona-based market-segmentation model became the standard way to target B2B buyer constituencies in high-tech sales.

5. *The New Strategic Selling* - Robert B. Miller, Stephen E. Heiman, and Tad Tuleja - William Morrow - Heiman's "conceptual selling" and buying-influence model (economic, user, technical buyers) shaped modern stakeholder-mapping practice.

6. [Adele Revella: Buyer Persona Institute](https://buyerpersona.com/) - Buyer Persona Institute - Research-based templates and interviews for building actionable, sales-ready buyer personas.

7. [HubSpot: How to Create Buyer Personas](https://blog.hubspot.com/marketing/buyer-personas-beginner-guide) - HubSpot - Step-by-step guide with templates for researching and documenting buyer personas.

8. [Salesforce: Account-Based Selling and Personas](https://www.salesforce.com/resources/articles/sales/account-based-selling/) - Salesforce - Practical guidance on mapping personas and segmentation to account-based sales motions.

9. [Gartner: B2B Buying Jobs](https://www.gartner.com/en/newsroom/press-releases/2020-07-21-gartner-says-b2b-buying-jobs-are-changing-and-sellers-must-adapt) - Gartner - Gartner's six "buying jobs" framework helps sellers identify who does what in complex buying committees.

10. [LinkedIn Sales Solutions: Persona-Based Selling](https://business.linkedin.com/sales-solutions/blog/sales-productivity/2017/persona-based-selling) - LinkedIn - Tactical advice on tailoring messaging and content to executive and functional buyer personas.
