# References: AI-Assisted Story Generation & Agents

1. [Large Language Model](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Large_language_model) - Wikipedia - Overview of transformer-based language models, training, and the generative capabilities used in story creation.

2. [Prompt Engineering](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Prompt_engineering) - Wikipedia - Techniques for structuring instructions to guide LLMs toward consistent, high-quality narrative outputs.

3. [Chatbot](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chatbot) - Wikipedia - History, architecture, and design considerations for conversational agents used in sales simulation and practice.

4. *Natural Language Processing with Python* - Steven Bird, Ewan Klein, and Edward Loper - O'Reilly Media - The NLTK team's hands-on introduction to NLP remains a widely cited foundation for understanding tokenization, parsing, and generation behind modern LLMs.

5. *The Hundred-Page Machine Learning Book* - Andriy Burkov - Andriy Burkov - A concise, frequently recommended primer on ML concepts that underpin recommendation, classification, and personalization in sales agents.

6. [OpenAI: Prompt Engineering Guide](https://platform.openai.com/docs/guides/prompt-engineering) - OpenAI - Official strategies for writing clear, specific prompts that produce reliable AI-generated content.

7. [Anthropic: Prompt Engineering Overview](https://docs.anthropic.com/en/docs/build-with-claude/prompt-engineering/overview) - Anthropic - Best practices for prompt clarity, examples, and iterative refinement with Claude.

8. [Google: Responsible AI Practices](https://ai.google/responsibility/responsible-ai-practices/) - Google - Guidelines on fairness, accountability, and transparency that apply to AI-generated sales content.

9. [Hugging Face: NLP Course](https://huggingface.co/learn/nlp-course/) - Hugging Face - Free course on modern NLP pipelines, transformers, and conversational AI fundamentals.

10. [MIT OpenCourseWare: Artificial Intelligence](https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/6-034-artificial-intelligence-fall-2010/) - MIT - Classic introductory AI course covering search, reasoning, and learning that underpin sales-agent dialogue systems.
