## Why This Mascot

Story the Lion represents confidence, leadership, and the ability to take control — all core principles of the Challenger Sales methodology. Lions are natural storytellers in their social groups, making them the perfect metaphor for teaching sales professionals how to craft compelling narratives. The friendly, playful personality makes complex sales concepts approachable, while the confident, strategic traits embody the Challenger mindset of challenging the status quo with powerful insights.
