## Why This Intelligent Textbook

B2B buying has changed faster than most sales training has. Buyers do most of
their research before they ever talk to a seller, and they decide in large
committees where each member has done their own research. A seller who shows
up with a feature list adds little. A seller who brings a new insight about the
buyer's business, told as a story the committee can repeat, is the one who
shapes the decision.

**What the research says about Challenger selling:**

- In the Corporate Executive Board (CEB) study of more than **6,000 sales
  representatives**, **nearly 40%** of star performers fit the Challenger
  profile, and in complex-solution sales that share rose to **54%**[^1]
- Relationship Builders, the profile many sales leaders invest in most, made
  up only **7%** of star performers overall[^2]
- More than **53% of B2B customer loyalty** is driven by the sales experience
  itself, not by brand, product, service, or price[^3]

**What buyers face today:**

- When B2B buyers are considering a purchase, they spend only **17% of that
  time** meeting with potential suppliers, and as little as **5–6%** with any
  one sales rep[^4]
- The typical buying group for a complex B2B solution involves **six to ten
  decision makers**, and **77% of buyers** describe their latest purchase as
  very complex or difficult[^4]
- Gartner predicts that by 2028, **60% of B2B seller work** will be done
  through conversational interfaces powered by generative AI, up from less than
  5% in 2023[^5]

**The workforce this affects:**

- The U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics counts **1,613,600 wholesale and
  manufacturing sales representative jobs** (2024), with about **142,100
  openings** projected each year through 2034[^6]

Behind these numbers are sellers who get a few minutes with a buying committee
and need those minutes to count. This textbook helps them make each one count.

This book takes a different approach from a traditional sales manual. It is
built on a **learning graph of 400 interconnected concepts**. Each concept is
introduced only after the concepts it depends on, so the Challenger
methodology, storytelling psychology, buyer personas, and AI-assisted story
generation build on each other chapter by chapter. Case studies come from
products that are hard to explain, such as graph databases, AI frameworks, and
intelligent textbook tools, because those are the sales where storytelling
matters most. **Interactive MicroSims** let you practice key skills in the
browser, such as mapping stakeholders, choosing an insight type, and comparing
sales profiles. The entire textbook is **open source and free**, with no
paywalls and no access codes.

## How to Use This Book

This textbook is designed for self-paced study by sales professionals, account
executives, sales engineers, and business development managers. Each chapter
builds on earlier material, so reading in order is recommended. The book
includes:

- **17 Chapters** covering Challenger fundamentals, storytelling psychology,
  buyer personas, objection handling, AI-assisted story generation, product
  case studies, story effectiveness analytics, delivery and coaching, story
  libraries, and a capstone project
- **Interactive MicroSims** that let you explore the five sales profiles,
  build customer priority maps, map stakeholders, and select insight types
- **Glossary** with definitions for key concepts
- **Learning Graph** showing the dependencies among 400 concepts
- **Search** available from any page using the search bar

The [Learning Graph](learning-graph/index.md) shows how concepts connect
across chapters. If you want to explore out of order, or check the
prerequisites for a specific topic, start there.

## About the Author

![Dan McCreary headshot](./img/dan-headshot-small.png){ width="150px" align="right" }

Dan McCreary is a semi-retired AI researcher, solution architect, and
educator who has spent more than three decades helping Fortune 100
organizations reason over massive datasets. At Optum he founded the
Generative AI Center of Excellence and led the team that built one of the
world's largest healthcare knowledge graphs — spanning over 25 billion
vertices — to unify member, provider, and patient insights. Dan's deep
background in knowledge representation and systems thinking underpins the
precise learning graphs and intelligent textbook workflows used throughout
this course.

He is the co-author of *Making Sense of NoSQL* (Manning Publications), the
founding chair of the NoSQL Now! conference, and a frequent keynote speaker
on semantic search, ontology strategy, and AI hardware. Beyond industry, Dan
has mentored students as a STEM volunteer since 2014 and now applies the same
rigor to building open educational resources. You can visit the
[Intelligent Textbooks Case Studies](https://dmccreary.github.io/intelligent-textbooks/case-studies/)
to see over 87 textbooks that Dan has created or co-created with other
authors.

**Selected Credentials**

enablement proposals, or other publications, please use one of the following
citation formats.

**APA (7th edition)**

**Chicago (17th edition)**

McCreary, Dan. 2026. *Challenger Selling*. https://dmccreary.github.io/challenger-selling/.

**MLA (9th edition)**

**BibTeX**
