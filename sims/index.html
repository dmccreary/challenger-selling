    List of MicroSims for Challenger Selling¶

    Interactive Micro Simulations to help students learn challenger selling fundamentals.

    • A/B Test Designer

      Learners design an A/B test of a price objection story by choosing one variable, writing a hypothesis, and picking a success metric, then decide whether early and later results are statistically significant.

    • Adaptive Learning Path Designer

      Learners read three student performance scenarios and route each student to a remedial, standard, or advanced learning path, with feedback on which signals drove the decision.

    • AI Capability Selector

      Learners match four sales-storytelling use cases to the AI capability that serves each: NLP, speech recognition, sentiment AI, or emotion AI.

    • Architecture Layer Designer

      Learners choose an integration, data, security, and scalability approach for a storytelling platform and see their architecture drawn as layers, with weak layers flagged.

    • Brand Alignment Checker

      Learners rate a story draft as aligned, neutral, or misaligned with four brand attributes, explain their reasoning, and compare it with an expert analysis and an on-brand rewrite.

    • Challenger Principles Explorer

      Learners reveal the three Challenger principles (teach, tailor, take control) with examples, see how they work together, and answer three scenario questions with one attempt each.

    • Channel Story Adapter

      Learners adapt one predictive-maintenance customer story for email, presentation, video, and social media by choosing the length, format, and focus that fit each channel.

    • CI/CD Pipeline Builder

      Learners choose the automated actions for the build, test, and deploy stages of a story library pipeline, avoiding manual steps and skipped tests, and see the pipeline drawn as a flow.

    • Cognitive Bias Simulator

      Scenario-based simulator in which learners identify the cognitive bias behind a buyer's hesitation and select the story strategy that addresses it, with explanatory feedback and a summary table.

    • Crisis Response Simulator

      Learners make four decisions in a data breach crisis and hear the press statement their choices produce, with each crisis-communication principle marked as met or missed.

    • Customer Priority Map Builder

      Learners rank five competing priorities for a CFO, CTO, and COO, compare each ranking with an expert map, then use the completed Customer Priority Map to judge where a teaching insight aligns or creates tension.

    • Dialogue Flow Designer

      Learners order six dialogue stages for a CFO sales-practice agent, choose transition conditions including a strong/weak coaching branch, and see a live flowchart of their design.

    • Framework Comparison Chart

      Learners review a comparison chart of TensorFlow, PyTorch, and Scikit-learn, then choose the best framework for three realistic selection scenarios.

    • Graph Data Model Visualizer

      Learners click each node and edge in a small Customer-Order-Product graph to reveal its type and properties, then answer five identification questions about the graph data model.

    • Graph Traversal Explorer

      Learners pick a start node and a traversal pattern (neighbors, within two hops, or shortest path), predict the result, then reveal it, with each hop compared to a relational JOIN.

    • Hero's Journey Interactive Map

      Click-to-explore circular map of the 11 stages of Campbell's Hero's Journey, each translated into a stage of a customer's buying journey, followed by a three-question comprehension check.

    • Immersive Experience Designer

      Learners choose VR, AR, or MR and three experience elements for a production-line showcase aimed at remote prospects, and get a specification with a technology-environment consistency check.

    • Industry Story Translator

      Learners adapt one story template for healthcare, financial services, and manufacturing by choosing industry language, the challenge that industry measures, and its regulatory reference.

    • Insight Personalizer

      Learners tailor one Challenger insight about legacy system costs for a CFO, a startup CTO, and a healthcare COO by choosing an emphasis and a supporting context, and see the rewritten insight.

    • Insight Type Selector

      Learners choose a Warmer, Rational Drowning, or Rock Star insight for three customer mindsets, explain their choice, and see an example of the right insight applied.

    • Integration Architect

      Learners choose which sales systems to integrate and route three story data flows between them, then see their architecture drawn as a diagram with valid and misrouted flows highlighted.

    • Learning Graph Viewer

      Interactive network of every concept in the course and its prerequisites, with search, category filters, and zoom to explore how ideas build on each other.

    • Learning Graph Visualizer

      Learners click each arrow in a four-concept learning graph to reveal the prerequisite relationship it encodes, then answer five questions about dependencies and valid learning order.

    • Memory Retention Experiment

      Step-through experiment in which learners read a story-based and a fact-based presentation of the same content, answer recall questions with both hidden, and compare what each format made memorable.

    • Metrics Cascade Designer

      Learners cascade a "grow revenue 20%" goal into two or three storytelling objectives and one or two KPIs for each, and see the cascade drawn with misaligned or vanity KPIs flagged.

    • Metrics Selector

      Learners match four business questions about story impact to the right metric family: engagement, retention, conversion, or pipeline velocity.

    • MicroSim Type Explorer

      Learners match five learning needs to the MicroSim type that serves them best: p5.js simulation, Chart.js chart, vis-network diagram, causal-loop diagram, or concept-classifier quiz.

    • Objection Story Selector

      Step-through practice in which learners match five common customer objections to the Price, Timing, Competitor, Risk or Authority story type and see an example story for each.

    • Persona Matcher

      Step-through practice in which learners identify the buyer persona behind three stakeholder descriptions and choose the messaging focus that will resonate with each.

    • Prompt Engineering Workshop

      Learners select, order and refine prompt elements to build an AI prompt that generates a sales story for a manufacturing CTO, with feedback on completeness, focus and structure.

    • ROI Calculator

      Learners total the costs and benefits of a story library, calculate a 360% ROI, then test how attribution, margin, and the hurdle rate change the conclusion.

    • Sales Profiles Comparison

      Learners reveal the five CEB sales profiles, compare their share of star performers in complex sales using verified research data, and answer three questions with one attempt each.

    • Sentiment Analyzer

      Learners label the sentiment of four customer excerpts, explain the indicator words, and see the indicators highlighted, including hedges in mixed sentiment.

    • Stakeholder Mapping Tool

      Learners categorize four university stakeholders by buyer type (economic, technical, user) and influence type (coach, mobilizer, skeptic, friend), then see whom to prioritize and how to engage each.

    • Story Arc Builder

      Drag-and-drop builder in which learners sequence four elements of an IoT customer story into Setup, Rising Action, Climax and Resolution, then see the resulting tension curve.

    • Story Component Builder

      Two-step builder in which learners sequence the six sales story components for a predictive maintenance sale, then write and review a complete story.

    • Story Customizer

      Learners decide which story elements to customize and how deeply (light, medium, or deep) for a healthcare CFO, a startup CTO, and a manufacturing CEO, and see each customized version.

    • Story Draft Builder

      Learners write each of the six components of a predictive-maintenance customer story following drafting tips, then see the assembled draft with a structural checklist and an example to compare.

    • Story Innovation Explorer

      Learners reinvent a standard "we cut costs 20%" story by choosing an unconventional structure, angle, format, and example, and see the resulting story concept.

    • Story Library Organizer

      Learners tag six customer stories by industry, persona, objection, deal stage, and product, and see each story become a searchable library card.

    • Story Process Flow

      Learners click eight shuffled story creation stages into order, name the criteria for three key gates, and see the complete process flow drawn with gates and a revision loop.

    • Story Tester

      Learners rate a vague story draft on clarity, relevance, effectiveness, and authenticity, write feedback for the author, and compare it with an expert review and an improved draft.

    • Strategic Alignment Mapper

      Learners map four storytelling initiatives to the business strategy each supports and see an alignment matrix that exposes coverage gaps.

    • Template Component Assembler

      Learners pick one component from each category of a story library and arrange them into a coherent story for a CFO about cutting IT costs through cloud migration.

    • Testing Strategy Selector

      Learners match four story-platform testing scenarios to unit, integration, system, or user acceptance testing.

    • Training Program Designer

      Learners design an 8-week storytelling training program by selecting 4-6 components, sequencing them, and choosing practice formats, then see the resulting weekly schedule.

    • Trust Story Selector

      Learners match four buyer trust concerns to the trust story type that answers each: credibility, authority, vulnerability, or authenticity.

    • Use Case Matcher

      Learners match four business scenarios to the graph database use case that solves them: social network analysis, fraud detection, recommendation engines, or knowledge graphs.

    • Vertical Insight Tailorer

      Learners adapt one legacy-systems Challenger insight for Healthcare, Financial Services and Technology by choosing a framing and messaging focus, then compare the rewritten insights.

    • xAPI Statement Builder

      Learners turn three plain-English learning events into xAPI statements by choosing the actor, verb, and object, and see the JSON statement they built after each submission.
