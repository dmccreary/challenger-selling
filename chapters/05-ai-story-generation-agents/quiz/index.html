    Quiz: AI-Assisted Story Generation & Agents¶

    Test your understanding of AI-assisted story generation, prompt engineering, ethical AI use, and interactive sales agents with these 10 review questions.

    1. What is AI hallucination in the context of sales storytelling?¶

    1. AI generating plausible but false or fabricated information with high confidence
    2. AI creating vivid visual images to accompany stories
    3. AI slowing down response times during story generation
    4. AI automatically detecting biased language in stories
    Show Answer

    The correct answer is A. AI hallucination occurs when models generate confident-sounding but inaccurate or fabricated content, such as fake statistics or customer quotes. The other options describe unrelated capabilities or behaviors and do not match the definition given in the chapter.

    Concept Tested: AI Hallucination

    2. Why is human-AI collaboration generally preferred over fully automated story generation for customer-facing sales content?¶

    1. Humans are always slower than AI at drafting narratives
    2. AI lacks the strategic insight, authenticity, and quality judgment that humans provide
    3. AI-generated content is never useful as a first draft
    4. Automated generation is legally prohibited in all sales contexts
    Show Answer

    The correct answer is B. The most effective workflow combines AI's speed and scale with human strategic oversight, fact-checking, and refinement. AI can produce useful drafts, but humans ensure the story is accurate, authentic, and aligned with the customer's context and business objectives.

    Concept Tested: Human-AI Collaboration

    3. Which prompt characteristic most improves the quality of AI-generated sales stories?¶

    1. Vague requests that give the AI maximum creative freedom
    2. Specific context, target persona, Challenger insight, structure, and tone
    3. A single-word prompt such as "story"
    4. Constraints that prevent the AI from mentioning the customer
    Show Answer

    The correct answer is B. Effective prompts provide focused context and clear instructions about the audience, insight, structure, and desired tone. Vague or overly broad prompts produce generic output, while excessive constraints can prevent the AI from producing a useful draft.

    Concept Tested: Prompt Engineering

    4. An AI generates a sales story that includes a specific ROI statistic from a named customer. What is the first step before using it with a prospect?¶

    1. Verify the statistic and customer detail against reliable sources
    2. Use it immediately because AI output is usually accurate
    3. Delete the story and start over with a simpler prompt
    4. Share it with the prospect to test its credibility
    Show Answer

    The correct answer is A. AI content verification requires checking factual claims before customer use. AI can confidently invent statistics, quotes, or company details, so verification protects credibility. Using unverified AI output, deleting useful drafts, or testing on prospects are not responsible practices.

    Concept Tested: AI Content Verification

    5. During a simulated sales call, the agent raises a price objection that the salesperson already addressed earlier. Which agent capability most likely failed?¶

    1. Intent recognition
    2. Entity extraction
    3. Natural language generation
    4. Context management
    Show Answer

    The correct answer is D. Context management maintains conversation state, including which objections have already been raised and resolved. A failure here makes the agent repeat itself unrealistically. Intent recognition, entity extraction, and natural language generation handle other aspects of understanding and responding.

    Concept Tested: Context Management

    6. What is a key benefit of using story prompt templates for AI-assisted generation?¶

    1. They eliminate the need for human review
    2. They make every generated story longer
    3. They ensure consistency and speed across the team
    4. They reduce the factual accuracy of outputs
    Show Answer

    The correct answer is C. Templates provide reusable structures with placeholders for persona, industry, insight, and tone, ensuring that team members generate stories with consistent quality and less effort. They do not remove the need for review or intentionally reduce accuracy.

    Concept Tested: Story Prompt Templates

    7. A sales agent needs to determine whether the salesperson is asking a discovery question or responding to a price objection. Which capability is primarily involved?¶

    1. Natural language generation
    2. Sentiment analysis
    3. Intent recognition
    4. Emotion detection
    Show Answer

    The correct answer is C. Intent recognition classifies what the user is trying to accomplish, such as asking a question or raising an objection. Natural language generation produces responses, sentiment analysis assesses tone, and emotion detection identifies specific feelings, but none of those classify the user's purpose.

    Concept Tested: Intent Recognition

    8. A generated story consistently portrays decision-makers as men and uses stereotypical language for certain industries. Which AI process should be applied?¶

    1. AI content verification
    2. Version control
    3. Prompt templating
    4. AI bias detection
    Show Answer

    The correct answer is D. AI bias detection identifies unfair stereotypes and unbalanced representation in generated content. While verification checks facts and templates provide structure, neither specifically addresses representational bias. Version control tracks changes but does not identify biased content.

    Concept Tested: AI Bias Detection

    9. Which process checks AI-generated sales stories for accuracy, consistency, and factual correctness before they are used with customers?¶

    1. AI content verification
    2. AI hallucination
    3. Entity extraction
    4. Context management
    Show Answer

    The correct answer is A. AI content verification is the process of validating accuracy, consistency, and factual correctness. AI hallucination is the problem being checked, while entity extraction and context management are agent capabilities unrelated to fact-checking generated stories.

    Concept Tested: AI Content Verification

    10. To make a sales simulation agent realistic and broadly useful, its training data should be:¶

    1. Limited to one industry to ensure simplicity
    2. Diverse and grounded in real customer interactions
    3. Purely synthetic so it can be controlled perfectly
    4. Focused only on successful outcomes
    Show Answer

    The correct answer is B. Diverse, realistic training data grounded in actual sales interactions produces agents that simulate varied personas, objections, and conversation paths. Limiting the data to one industry, using only synthetic examples, or excluding challenging outcomes reduces realism and learning value.

    Concept Tested: Agent Training Data
